Our Services

  • Tyre Pyrolysis Oil – Cost-effective industrial fuel for boilers, furnaces and kilns.
  • Carbon Black – Recovered carbon for rubber, tyres, plastics, coatings and roads.
  • Steel Wire – Recovered from tyre beads and belts and sent for metal recycling.
  • Recyclable Gas – Uncondensed gas reused in-house, making our process self-sufficient.

Our Process in Action:

We turn discarded tyres into valuable resources for industry:

  • Tyre Collection – From tyre dealers, transport fleets, workshops and municipalities.
  • Pre-processing – Cleaning and cutting tyres to prepare them for the reactor.
  • Thermal Pyrolysis – Conversion in oxygen-free reactors.
  • Resource Recovery – Producing: Fuel Oil, Carbon Black, Steel Wire, Recyclable Gas
