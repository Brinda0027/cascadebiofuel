ABOUT US

At Cascade Biofuels Pvt Ltd, we transform a global challenge into an opportunity. Using state-of-the-art pyrolysis technology, we convert plastic waste into clean fuels, carbon black, and reusable by-products.


  • OUR MISSION
    Our Mission is to help industries and communities reduce waste, cut costs, and transition towards sustainable fuel alternatives.

    OUR VISION
    To become a global leader in renewable fuels, building a cleaner tomorrow through innovation, responsibility, and impact.

    OUR VALUES
    • Integrity    • Sustainability    • Innovation   
    • Responsibility    • Community Impact

Services

Featured Srvices

Plastic waste management

collect plastic waste and convert it to fuel oil through pyrolysis. The final product of the pyrolysis plant is mainly fuel oil (can be further refined into diesel) and uncondensed gas.

Tyre waste Management

collect and convert tyre wastes into fuel oil through pyrolysis. The final product of the pyrolysis plant is mainly fuel oil,carbon black and uncondensed gas

Why Partner with Us?

Partnering with Cascade Biofuels is not just about sourcing materials—it’s about future-proofing your business.

  • ● Sustainability Advantage – Meet your ESG targets and showcase eco-responsibility to stakeholders.
  • ● Cost Savings – Access affordable fuel substitutes compared to fluctuating fossil fuel markets.
  • ● Regulatory Compliance – Stay aligned with India’s waste management & renewable energy policies.
  • ● Innovation Edge – Be among the first to adopt cutting-edge, scalable waste-to-fuel technology.
  • ● Brand Reputation – Enhance your corporate image by being part of the green energy revolution.
